Supplier Quality Review, First Half 2026

Northbank Precision Ltd, Procurement and Quality team. Issued on 14 July 2026 to the operations leadership group.

# Purpose and Scope

This review covers the six direct-material suppliers that delivered more than 50 shipments to the Sheffield and Coventry plants between January and June 2026.[[1]](#footnote-1)

Delivery performance is measured against the confirmed delivery date. Defect rates are given in *parts per million* (ppm) of received parts.

# Summary of Results

Average on-time delivery improved from **91.4 percent** in the first quarter to **93.8 percent** in the second quarter, while the average defect rate fell from 412 to 365 ppm.

## Key Findings

* Delivery performance improved at every supplier in the second quarter.
  + Bexley Fasteners gained 3.2 points after moving to weekly call-offs.
  + Dunmore Electronics gained 3.8 points but is still below the 90 percent target.
    - Most late shipments were linked to a single connector family.
* Defect rates fell at five of the six suppliers.
  + Corran Plastics was the exception, rising from 365 to 372 ppm.
* No supplier was moved to a lower rating during the period.

# Corrective Actions

The following actions were agreed at the supplier review meeting on 8 July 2026:

1. Dunmore Electronics to submit a revised control plan by 31 July 2026.
   1. The plan must cover incoming inspection of the connector family.
   2. Northbank quality engineers will audit the plan on site in August.
2. Bexley Fasteners to extend weekly call-offs to the Coventry plant.
3. Procurement to renew the Arden Castings and Elstree Coatings agreements for two years.
