# Purpose and Scope

This policy sets out how employees book business travel, which costs the company pays and how expenses are reported and approved.

It applies to travel paid with the company card and to expenses that employees pay personally and claim back.

# Booking Travel

All flights, hotels and rental cars must be booked through the corporate travel portal unless the travel desk confirms in writing that the portal cannot be used.

## Air Travel

1.  Book economy class for flights shorter than six hours.
    1.  Premium economy may be booked for flights of six hours or more.
    2.  Business class requires prior approval by a vice president.
        1.  Attach the written approval to the booking request.
2.  Book at least 14 days before departure whenever possible.
3.  Choose the lowest logical fare within two hours of the preferred departure time.

## Hotels and Meals

Hotel rates may not exceed the nightly cap for the city tier shown in Table 1.

Table 1. Hotel caps and meal allowances by city tier (USD)

| City tier | Hotel cap per night Standard | Hotel cap per night Peak season | Daily meal allowance |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Tier 1: New York, San Francisco, Boston | $325 | $410 | $95 |
| Tier 2: Chicago, Seattle, Denver | $260 | $320 | $80 |
| Tier 3: all other U.S. cities | $190 | $230 | $70 |
| International | $280 | $350 | $90 |

Peak season caps apply during the trade show weeks listed on the travel portal.

## Ground Transportation

-   Use public transport or a shared shuttle when it is practical.
-   Rental cars must be intermediate size or smaller.
    -   Decline the optional collision damage waiver in the United States.
    -   Return the car with a full tank to avoid refueling charges.
-   Mileage for personal vehicles is reimbursed at the published IRS rate.

# Expense Reporting

Submit each expense report within **30 days** of returning from the trip. Receipts are required for every item over _$25_.

1.  Photograph each receipt in the expense app on the day you receive it.
2.  Assign every line to a project code or cost center.
3.  Submit the report to your manager for the first approval.

# Non-Reimbursable Expenses

-   Upgrades to airline seats, hotel rooms or rental cars paid for personal comfort.
-   Traffic fines, parking tickets and towing charges.
-   Minibar items, in-room movies and spa services.
-   Travel costs of family members or guests who join the trip.
