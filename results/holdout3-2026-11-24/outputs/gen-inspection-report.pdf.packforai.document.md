### Findings

1. Seven smoke detectors on the second floor failed the sensitivity test and must be replaced.
2. Three sprinkler heads in the loading dock are painted over and must be replaced.
3. Five emergency lights did not stay on for the full 90 minutes.
4. One exit sign above the east stairwell door is not illuminated.
5. Two fire extinguishers in the warehouse are overdue for their annual service.
