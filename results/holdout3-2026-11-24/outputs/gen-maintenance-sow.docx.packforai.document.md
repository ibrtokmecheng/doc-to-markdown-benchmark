Statement of Work: Preventive Maintenance Services

_Prepared for Larkspur Business Park Owners Association by Corvane Facility Services, LLC_

**Agreement reference:** MSA-2026-118, statement of work 4, version 2

# 1\. Purpose

This statement of work describes the preventive maintenance services that Corvane Facility Services, LLC will provide at Larkspur Business Park from April 1, 2027 to March 31, 2028.

It forms part of the master services agreement and uses the terms defined there.

# 2\. Scope of Services

Corvane will maintain the mechanical, electrical and life safety systems of the four buildings listed below.

## 2.1 Buildings Covered

-   Building A, 2200 Larkspur Drive: offices, 48,500 square feet
    -   Includes the rooftop chiller plant that serves Buildings A and B
-   Building B, 2210 Larkspur Drive: offices and a fitness center, 36,200 square feet
-   Building C, 2300 Larkspur Drive: light industrial, 61,750 square feet
    -   The tenant maintains its own process equipment and compressed air
-   Building D, 2310 Larkspur Drive: data center and offices, 29,900 square feet

## 2.2 Maintenance Schedule

Table 1 lists the planned visits and labor hours for each building system.

Table 1. Planned preventive maintenance by system

| System | Main tasks | Planned effort Visits per year | Planned effort Hours per visit | Responsible |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| HVAC air handlers | Replace filters Check belts and bearings | 12 | 6 | Corvane |
| Chillers and cooling towers | Inspect and log operating data Clean condenser tubes | 4 | 16 | Corvane |
| Boilers | Combustion analysis Safety valve test | 2 | 8 | Corvane |
| Fire alarm and sprinklers | Annual inspection and testing | 1 | 40 | Licensed subcontractor |
| Emergency generators | Load bank test Fluid and battery checks | 4 | 5 | Corvane |
| Elevators | Monthly safety check | 12 | 3 | Elevator contractor |

_Note: Hours per visit are averages for all four buildings; actual hours are reported monthly._

### 2.2.1 Seasonal Tasks

1.  Switch the cooling towers to winter operation in October.
2.  Flush and test the heating water loops before November 15.
3.  Inspect roof drains and gutters after the leaves have fallen.

# 3\. Deliverables

Corvane will deliver the following documents to the owners association:

1.  A monthly service report by the fifth business day of each month.
    1.  Completed and overdue work orders listed by building.
    2.  Labor hours compared with the plan in Table 1.
2.  An annual condition assessment of all major equipment.
3.  Updated equipment records in the owner's maintenance system.

# 5\. Assumptions and Exclusions

-   The owner provides access to all plant rooms and roofs during business hours.
-   Repairs found during maintenance are quoted separately and need written approval.
-   Tenant equipment, furniture and IT systems are excluded from this scope.
