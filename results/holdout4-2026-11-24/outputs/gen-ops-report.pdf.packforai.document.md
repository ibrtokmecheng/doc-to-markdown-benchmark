### Summary

The Lakeland center shipped 41,862 orders in March, 2.6 percent more than planned. Pick accuracy was 99.6 percent on the day and swing shifts but fell to 99.1 percent at night. Two dock doors were closed for repairs for nine days, which delayed inbound receiving.

### Issues

1. New pickers on the night shift made most of the picking errors in the frozen aisles.
2. Dock doors 7 and 8 were out of service from March 11 to March 19.
3. Inbound trailers waited an average of 74 minutes for a door during that period.
4. Fuel surcharges rose for the third month in a row.
