## Slide 4: Top Customer Accounts

- Harbor Point Grocers renewed its contract for three years
  - Its volume commitment rises 12 percent in 2028
- Valle Verde Restaurants added four new locations
- Two hospital accounts moved to a competitor
- Credit terms were tightened for three accounts
  - Days sales outstanding fell from 41 to 36

**Speaker notes:** Both hospital accounts cited a group purchasing agreement that we could not match on price.

## Slide 5: Product Line Trends

- Frozen seafood sales grew 15.2 percent on new restaurant accounts.
- Dairy sales were flat because milk prices fell during the quarter.
- Bakery sales fell 4.6 percent after a supplier recall in November.
- Fresh produce share of revenue: 31.7%

## Slide 7: Plan for the First Quarter
