Travel and Expense Policy

_Issued by the Finance Department of Brightwater Provisions, Inc. for all employees and contractors_

**Policy owner:** Corporate Controller, Finance Department

# 1\. Purpose and Scope

This policy explains how employees book business travel, which costs Brightwater Provisions reimburses and how expense reports are approved.

It applies to every trip taken on company business, including customer visits, supplier audits and training courses.

# 2\. Booking Travel

## 2.1 Air and Rail

-   Book economy class through the company travel portal at least 14 days before departure
    -   Premium economy is allowed on flights longer than six hours
-   Choose rail instead of air for trips under 250 miles when the rail journey takes less than five hours
-   Keep the booking confirmation and the boarding pass for the expense report
    -   Electronic copies are accepted in place of paper receipts

### 2.2.1 Extended Stays

1.  Ask the travel desk for a corporate apartment when a stay is longer than 14 nights.
2.  Compare the apartment rate with the hotel limit for the same city tier.
3.  Attach the approved comparison to the first expense report of the stay.

# 4\. Submitting Expense Reports

1.  Enter every expense in the expense system within 30 days of returning from the trip.
    1.  Photograph each receipt of $25 or more and attach it to the expense line.
    2.  Explain any expense that is above the limits in this policy.
2.  Submit the completed report to your manager for approval.
3.  Finance reviews approved reports and pays them with the next payroll run.

# 5\. Expenses Not Reimbursed

-   Fines for traffic or parking violations, even when the vehicle was used for work.
-   Upgrades to business or first class that were not approved in advance.
-   Travel costs of family members or friends who join the trip.
