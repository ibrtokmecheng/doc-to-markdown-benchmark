### Summary

Plant 2 recorded four recordable injuries in the third quarter, one fewer than in the second quarter. The only lost-time injury was a hand laceration in the machining bay on August 14.

No environmental releases or regulatory inspections were reported during the quarter.

### Corrective Actions

1. Replace the cut-resistant gloves in the machining bay with a higher-rated model. a. Order the new gloves before October 20. b. Train all machinists on the new glove sizes.
2. Add a second guard interlock on the deburring station.
3. Repeat the forklift pedestrian-zone audit in the warehouse every month. a. Publish the audit results on the safety board.
