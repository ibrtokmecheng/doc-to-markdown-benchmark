# What Changed This Quarter
Improvements
Open issues
Live chat launched for all customers on July 8
Staffed by four agents during business hours
Knowledge base articles were rewritten for the top 40 questions
Email backlog peaked at 312 tickets in August
Warranty claims still need two handoffs
A shared queue with the quality team is being tested

### Notes:
The August email backlog followed the loss of two senior agents; both positions are now filled.

<!-- Slide number: 5 -->
# Customer Satisfaction
Satisfaction rose on every channel except email, where replies slowed in August.
Live chat started in July and already has the highest score of all channels.
Detractors most often mentioned repeated transfers between teams.
Overall CSAT in the third quarter: 89.0%

# Priorities for the Fourth Quarter
Bring the median email response under four hours
Add a second shift for email on Mondays and Tuesdays
Route warranty claims directly to the quality team
Target a single handoff for every warranty claim

### Notes:
Budget for the second email shift was approved by the operations director on September 22.
