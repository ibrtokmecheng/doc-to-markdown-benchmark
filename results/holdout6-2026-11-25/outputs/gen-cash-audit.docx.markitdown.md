# Executive Summary

Internal Audit reviewed how front desk cash is received, counted, stored and deposited at three hotels during the first quarter of 2031.

**Overall rating:** Needs improvement. Two of the six findings are rated high.

Management agreed with every finding and has set target dates for all corrective actions, the last of them in November 2031.

# Scope and Approach

The audit covered the following procedures at each hotel:

* Opening and closing counts of each cash drawer
* Access to the back office safe and its key log
* Preparation and timeliness of bank deposits
* Reconciliation of deposits to the property management system

The detailed test steps are described in the [<u>Audit IA-2031-07 test plan</u>](https://intranet.halcyon-harbor.example/audit/ia-2031-07/test-plan), which is available to hotel controllers.

# Findings

Each finding is rated using the scale in Table 1.

Table 1. Rating scale

| **Rating** | **Meaning** | **Expected action** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| High | A control is missing or does not work, and a loss is likely | Correct within 30 days |
| Medium | A control works only part of the time | Correct within 90 days |
| Low | A minor weakness with little exposure | Correct at the next policy review |

## Repeat Findings from 2030

Two findings from the 2030 audit were found again:

1. Blind counts were not performed at the Seattle Waterfront hotel.
2. Late deposits exceeded the target of 5 percent of all deposits.

Management response: we accept the findings and will report progress to the Audit Committee every quarter until all actions are closed.
