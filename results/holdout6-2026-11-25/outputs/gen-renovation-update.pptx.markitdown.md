# Risks and Mitigations
Furniture lead times have grown to 22 weeks
Orders for the 2032 hotels go out in October
The union agreement limits weekend work at Portland Pearl
Fixed-price contracts cover 80 percent of the remaining budget
Change orders above $50,000 need committee approval
A 6 percent contingency is held at program level

<!-- Slide number: 6 -->
# Lessons from the Pilot Hotel
Guest complaints about noise fell after work hours moved to 10 am
Opening rooms floor by floor kept occupancy above 82 percent
Early mock-up rooms caught three design errors before ordering
Seattle Waterfront was the pilot hotel and finished two weeks ahead of schedule.
