Q3 2026 Operations Review

Fernhill Logistics, Distribution Division. Prepared by the Operations Analytics team on 2026-10-06 for the quarterly business review.

# Executive Summary

Third-quarter throughput reached **1.15 million parcels**, an increase of *6.8 percent* over the second quarter. On-time delivery held at **97.1 percent** despite two weeks of severe weather in September, and the cost per parcel fell from GBP 2.52 to GBP 2.41.

This review covers the three regional hubs in Leeds, Bristol and Glasgow. All figures are drawn from the warehouse management system and the carrier settlement reports.

# Network Performance

## Warehouse Throughput

Monthly volumes rose at every hub. Leeds remained the busiest site, while Glasgow grew fastest after its second sorting line opened in July.

## Delivery Reliability

Late deliveries remain concentrated in a small number of causes. The breakdown below is *provisional* until carrier claims for September are settled.

* Late deliveries fell to 2.9 percent of all parcels.
  + Weather delays accounted for 41 percent of late parcels.
  + Carrier handover errors accounted for 23 percent.
* Damage claims dropped to 0.4 percent of parcels.
  + Glasgow introduced reinforced tote liners in August.
* Complaints per 10,000 parcels fell from 3.1 to 2.6.

# Action Plan

The following actions were agreed at the operations meeting on 2026-10-02, in priority order:

1. Extend the Leeds night shift to cover Saturday peaks from 2026-11-02.
2. Replace the Bristol sorter belt drives before the December surge.
3. Roll out reinforced tote liners to the Leeds and Bristol hubs.
4. Review the carrier handover checklist with all regional managers.
