Parcel Sorting Automation Study Report OE-2026-14
Automating Parcel Sorting at Regional Hubs
Operations Engineering Group, Fernhill Logistics. November 2026.
Summary. This study compares four ways to raise sorting capacity at the Leeds, Bristol and Glasgow hubs
ahead of the 2027 peak season. It recommends a phased cross-belt installation at Leeds, a robotic put wall at
Glasgow, and continued manual sorting with handheld scanners at Bristol until volumes justify a larger
investment.
